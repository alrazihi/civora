## 2. Proof Scope

This stage proves that the CIVORA AI module is:

1. **Process-independent** — works across two genuinely different assistance processes with different forms, fields, evidence, rules, workflows, and decisions
2. **Tenant-isolated** — Organization A cannot access Organization B's cases, documents, evidence, forms, submissions, rules, evaluations, decisions, or audit events
3. **AI-disablable** — core CIVORA continues functioning when AI is disabled via `NoopProvider` / `CIVORA_AI_ENABLED=false`

### 2.1 Processes Tested

| Process | Organization | Workflow | Forms | Evidence | Rules | Decisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency Assistance | Org A | Intake → Assessment → Review → Approved/Rejected | Household intake (size, income, housing, urgency) | ID document, Proof of residence | Urgency-based eligibility | Approved/Rejected |
| Education Assistance | Org B | Applied → DocumentReview → Interview → Awarded/Rejected → Enrolled | Education grant application (GPA, major, prior degree, income) | Transcript, Personal essay | GPA and eligibility rules | Approved/Rejected |

No process names are hardcoded in implementation. The tests use dynamically created workflow definitions, forms, rules, and evidence.
